Documenting Until Death

Collecting is a part of who I am as an individual. What you see here is a collection of Post Its that I've written and collected in the past 3 years of university.The first documented date is "13 Nov 2023". The last one is "11.12.2025". I saved these sticky notes because I was afraid I would forget who I was and who I will be. I cling to every bit of paper that could bring me back to who I am as a person. The action of writing a post it note, peeling it off and then just being able to move it around or discard yourself of it is almost a ritualistic part. It is satisfyingly tactile. It is a good way to document everyday life. I will always document.

Until death.
